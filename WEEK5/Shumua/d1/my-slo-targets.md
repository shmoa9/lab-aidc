## SLI 1

Indicator: Successful scrape rate

Panel: Scrape Success %

Unit: percent

Target: >= 99%

Window: 5 minutes

Observed: 100%

Evidence: Grafana Team service dashboard, Scrape Success % panel, observed during the traffic test.

Why it fits: Confirms that Prometheus can successfully scrape the serving service and that monitoring data is available.

Limitations: Short measurement period; longer observation is needed to confirm the target over time.

## SLI 2

Indicator: P95 time to first token

Panel: P95 Time to First Token

Unit: seconds

Target: <= 0.05 seconds

Window: 5 minutes

Observed: approximately 0.038 seconds

Evidence: Grafana Team service dashboard, P95 Time to First Token panel, observed during the traffic test.

Why it fits: Measures the latency users experience before receiving the first generated token.

Limitations: Measurement is based on the observed traffic window and should be retested with different workloads and concurrency levels.
